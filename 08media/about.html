About Us

Welcome to STTWMP

Our Story

STTWMP is a website dedicated to helping people survive the 1-week Minecraft phase. We provide resources, tips, and support for those who are struggling with the game. Our goal is to help people enjoy Minecraft without getting burned out.

You can download our brochure and share this condensed information with your friends.

Tips and Tricks

Motivation Strategy Purpose Minecraft Example
Build Smaller Avoid overwhelming projects Build one room instead of an entire base
Make a Rough Draft Reduce perfectionism Build a basic structure before decorating
Automate Resources Reduce repetitive grinding Create an automatic crop farm
Plan Ahead Make returning easier Leave materials ready for your next build
Add Something Silly Keep gameplay entertaining Build a tiny museum or funny statue
